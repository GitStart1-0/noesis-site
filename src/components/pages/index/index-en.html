How the content works

A question does not end with an answer

Most quiz apps end the interaction immediately after an answer. Mythos: Quiz adds one more step: an explanation shown regardless of the result, together with a source for the fact.

Some tasks go beyond a single correct answer. Matching, arranging a sequence and comparing texts ask you to connect information rather than simply recall it.

Example

“Why were ancient statues, which we now imagine as white marble, originally brightly painted?”

Explanation: pigments on marble eroded over time; modern research detects traces of colour. Source: an open archaeological publication.

Content is reviewed by subject specialists before publication. This cannot eliminate every mistake, but it moves most corrections into review rather than after release.
